Your pool's fill water in Northeast Florida: hardness by utility

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Pool Resurfacing Match

Short answer

Short answer: Most Northeast Florida tap water is hard: JEA's averages run from about 120 ppm (7 gpg) to 372 ppm (22 gpg) by ZIP, and Atlantic Beach reports 300 mg/L. A new finish is filled with this water, so ask your resurfacer how they will start up and balance the pool for your source.

  1. Published figures

Published figures

JEA by ZIP: 32081 372 ppm; 32082 346 ppm; 32092 307 ppm; 32259 305 ppm; 32250 292 ppm; 32073 128 ppm; 32065 120 ppm. Atlantic Beach: 300 mg/L. St. Augustine and St. Johns County's CR 214 plant use reverse osmosis; Clay County Utility Authority, Fernandina Beach and Orange Park describe aeration and chlorination of Floridan Aquifer well water.[1][2][3][4][5][6][7]
