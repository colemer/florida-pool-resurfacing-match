Pool resurfacing around Orlando: permits, water and costs (2026)

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Pool Resurfacing Match

Short answer

Short answer: Around Orlando, pool work runs through city permitting: Kissimmee lists Pool/Hot Tub/Jacuzzi/Spa as its own permit type, Winter Garden requires a Zoning Compliance Review with an impervious-surface worksheet, and Oviedo wants the contractor's Florida license and insurance certificates naming the city. Angi's 2026 national average for resurfacing is about $11,000 (most pay $6,000 to $15,000).

  1. Permits
  2. Water
  3. What it costs

Permits

Kissimmee: Pool/Hot Tub/Jacuzzi/Spa construction is its own permit type with the Permitting Division (407-518-2379).

Winter Garden: a pool needs a Zoning Compliance Review with a survey or site plan and an ISR worksheet.

Oviedo: residential permit applications need a copy of the contractor's Florida license and insurance certificates naming the City of Oviedo.

Sanford: residential permits generally take up to 30 calendar days to clear every reviewing department.

Lake Mary: if a pool project removes a tree more than 15 feet from the structure, an arbor permit is required.[1][2][3][4][5]

Water

Most systems here pump Floridan Aquifer water treated for hydrogen sulfide: ozone in Winter Park, aeration in Oviedo, Toho and Winter Garden. Sanford adds polyphosphate for corrosion control. Several reports list no hardness value, so have your fill water tested before start-up.[6][7][8][9][10]

What it costs

Angi's 2026 national figures: about $11,000 on average, most paying $6,000 to $15,000; replaster about $5,500 and aggregate about $6,500 per 1,000 sq ft of surface; draining, cleaning and refilling about $425 to $475.[11]

Cost ranges

Angi's 2026 data puts the U.S. average for pool resurfacing at about $11,000, with a typical range of $6,000 to $15,000 depending on pool size and finish.[11]

Frequently asked questions

Does Winter Park count a pool toward impervious coverage?

Yes. Winter Park's 50% single-family impervious cap counts the house, driveway, pool, patios, A/C pads and artificial turf.[12]

How do I check a pool contractor's license?

Use DBPR's free Verify a License search; Florida puts pool interior refinishing in the pool/spa contractor scope.[13][14]
