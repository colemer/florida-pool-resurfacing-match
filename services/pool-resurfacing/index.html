Pool Resurfacing & Replastering in Florida

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Pool Resurfacing Match

Short answer: Resurfacing replaces the pool's interior finish (plaster, quartz or pebble). The pool is drained and prepared first. In Florida that is pool/spa contractor work.[1][2]

In Florida, interior refinishing and equipment work are pool/spa contractor work. Ask for the company's Florida license number and look it up on DBPR.

What to know

  • Section 489.105(3) lists interior refinishing and draining a pool for renovation in the pool/spa servicing contractor scope.
  • Ask for the Florida license number and check it on DBPR.
