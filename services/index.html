Pool resurfacing services

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Pool Resurfacing Match

  • Pool Resurfacing & Replastering: Resurfacing replaces the pool's interior finish (plaster, quartz or pebble).
  • Tile & Coping: Waterline tile and coping are often replaced during resurfacing because the pool is already drained..
  • Leak & Crack Repair: Leaks and structural cracks should be found and fixed before a new finish goes on..
  • Pool Equipment (pump, filter, heater): Replacing or repairing existing pool equipment, and adding heaters, is pool/spa contractor work in Florida; a simple filter change is excluded..

Florida Pool Resurfacing Match is a free referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform pool resurfacing and repair or any construction work. With the homeowner’s consent, we pass a request to up to 3 independent local companies named on our form (none are listed yet, so no requests are shared). Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted. Each contractor is responsible for its own licensing, insurance, pricing and work. You are never obligated to hire anyone.
