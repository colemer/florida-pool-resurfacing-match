What to look for in a pool contractor

Four checks before you sign anything. How our matching works.

Check 1 · License

Check the pool contractor license

  • Interior refinishing and substantial draining for repair fall within Florida's pool/spa contractor scope
  • Look the license number up on DBPR's free Verify a License search
  • Ask whether your city requires a permit for the job

Source[2][3]

Check 2 · Insurance

Proof of insurance

  • Ask for a current certificate of general liability insurance in the company's name
  • Ask whether the crew working at your home is covered by workers' compensation
  • Call the insurer on the certificate to confirm the policy is active

General guidance

Check 3 · Estimate

A written, itemized estimate

  • Scope, materials and quantities in writing, not just a total
  • Who applies for any permit, and who pays the permit fee
  • Start date, expected timeline, cleanup and haul-away

General guidance

Check 4 · Payment

Fair payment terms and a warranty

  • Be wary of paying most of the price before work starts
  • Tie payments to finished stages of the job
  • Get the workmanship and product warranties in writing

Frequently asked questions

Is this a pool company?

No. Florida Pool Resurfacing Match is a free referral service. With your consent, we can pass your request to up to 3 independent local pool contractors named on our form.

Does a pool resurfacer need a Florida license?

Interior refinishing and substantial draining for renovation are in Florida's pool/spa contractor scope (s. 489.105(3)). Check the license on DBPR.[2][3]

What does pool resurfacing cost?

Angi's 2026 national average is about $11,000, typically $6,000 to $15,000.[1]
