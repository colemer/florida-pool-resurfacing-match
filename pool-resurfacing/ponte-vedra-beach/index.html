Recent storms in this area

Storms bring debris, power outages and runoff into pools; these are the recent events recorded for this area.

  • Hurricane Irma (September 11, 2017): Homes along Roscoe Boulevard, which backs onto the Intracoastal Waterway, woke to surge flooding, downed trees and power lines.[1]

  • Hurricane Nicole (November 10, 2022): State Road A1A in South Ponte Vedra Beach washed out; FDEP measured about 465,000 cubic yards of sand lost along Ponte Vedra across Ian and Nicole, with moderate to major erosion along the developed shoreline.[3][4][5]

Who issues permits in Ponte Vedra Beach?

St. Johns County Building Services. Phone: 904-827-6800. Ponte Vedra Beach is unincorporated, so the county issues permits. The county requires a permit to construct, alter, repair or demolish structures and for regulated electrical, plumbing, gas and mechanical work. Many neighborhoods also have HOA architectural review for exterior changes.[7]

Florida defines interior refinishing, equipment replacement and draining a pool for repair as pool/spa contractor work. Ask the office whether your resurfacing or equipment job needs a permit before work starts.
