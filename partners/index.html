Partner companies

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Pool Resurfacing Match

These are the independent companies that may contact you, but only if they are named in the consent box when you send your request and you check that box. Each is a separate business responsible for its own licensing, insurance and work. Participating companies may pay us.

No partner companies are listed yet. That means we share requests with no one. We read each request and reply to the homeowner ourselves. A company listed later will never receive a request sent before it was named in the consent box.

How a company gets listed

  • We look up its Florida license on the DBPR license search and record the number and date (or note that its work needs no state license). That is the only check we do.
  • We never share requests with public adjusters.
  • Each company is named in the form's consent box for the areas it serves, so you see exactly who may contact you before you send.

Florida Pool Resurfacing Match is a free referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform pool resurfacing and repair or any construction work. With the homeowner’s consent, we pass a request to up to 3 independent local companies named on our form (none are listed yet, so no requests are shared). Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted. Each contractor is responsible for its own licensing, insurance, pricing and work. You are never obligated to hire anyone.
